# Tide

A tiny water tracker for iPhone. Your day is a moonlit sea that rises as you drink.

- **Hold anywhere** to pour. The longer you hold, the faster it flows. Let go to log it, or slide up to the top to cancel.
- **Tap a pebble** (sip / glass / mug / bottle) to drop in a set amount. You can rename and resize them in settings.
- **High-tide mark**: a dotted line on the water. Past it, the sea turns ink-violet. It shows you when a day runs high. It isn't a goal.
- **Tides** (or tap the moon) shows daily totals, averages, and the hours you drink most.
- The moon is tonight's real moon phase.
- Optional: water sounds that rise in pitch as the sea fills, and tilt (the water stays level when you tip the phone).

No accounts, no servers, no database. Everything is stored on the phone (localStorage) and works offline. Use **Settings → save a backup** now and then.

## Put it on your Home Screen
1. Host this folder anywhere static (GitHub Pages: repo **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch**, choose the branch and `/ (root)`).
2. Open the URL in **Safari** on iPhone → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
